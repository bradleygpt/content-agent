The 4-of-4 frozen-evidence result is the ADOPTION evidence, not the settled question. N=4 on one
session's evidence is exactly the sample size this publication tells readers not to trust: a house
whose thesis is "five events is a pattern in anecdotes, not a law" does not get to conclude "the note
task solves invention" from four drafts. The confirming sample is the live nightlies.

nightly actually runs. PASS = the note reached `pending` on the run, retries included, which is the
production-relevant unit. Rounds I force by hand are not counted — only unattended nightly runs.
